# PREFERENCES.md

## Topics wanted

- Software engineering: practical design, reliability, security, and developer tools.
- Artificial intelligence: model capabilities, evaluation, deployment, and societal impact.
- Self-hosting and internet infrastructure: open systems, privacy, and durable personal tools.
- Thoughtful analysis of technology, business, and policy when it explains real-world effects.

## Topics to skip

- Celebrity coverage, partisan punditry, outrage cycles, and repetitive breaking-news updates.
- Press-release rewrites, listicles, engagement bait, and unsupported hot takes.
- Pure product announcements without useful technical or practical context.

## Sources trusted

- No source is trusted by default. Add sources only after sustained useful coverage.

This is the initial cold-start profile. The future nightly preference workflow proposes
complete replacements for human review; it must never edit the accepted file in place.
